How we build

Edinexa Technologies is an education technology company founded by an educator. Every product starts from a simple test: would a busy teacher actually trust it and use it tomorrow?

Teachers first

Tools for professional growth, not surveillance. A teacher's coaching stays theirs unless they choose to share it.

Honest by design

If our tools don't know something, they say so. Missing data is never quietly turned into a score.

Privacy as a default

No selling data, no advertising, no tracking cookies. We collect only what the service needs to work.
