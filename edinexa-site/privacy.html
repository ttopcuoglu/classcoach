Effective September 19, 2026

This page explains how Edinexa Technologies LLC ("Edinexa," "we," "us") handles information when you visit edinexa.com.

Our products have their own policies

If you use Wivoza, its Terms of Use and Privacy Notice describe how your account, recordings, and coaching content are handled. That notice controls for the Wivoza app and website.

What this website collects

edinexa.com is an informational site. It has no accounts or forms, and it does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them.

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children through this site.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have sent us by writing to hello@edinexa.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.
